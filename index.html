A kitchen that never left the village

Panta Ilish opened in 2019 with one stove and one family recipe book. We still grind our mustard by hand and buy our fish from the morning market.

Nothing here is rushed. Curries simmer until the oil rises, rice is steamed in clay, and every plate comes out the way you would be served at home.
